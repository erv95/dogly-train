Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 3, 2026

Dogly Train ("we", "us", "our") operates the Dogly Train mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our app.

2. Third-Party Services

  • Firebase (Google) — Authentication, database, file storage, cloud functions. Firebase Privacy
  • Stripe — Payment processing for coin purchases. We do not store your card details. Stripe Privacy
  • PayPal — Alternative payment processing. PayPal Privacy
  • Expo — Push notification delivery. Expo Privacy

3. Data Security

All data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Firestore security rules enforce access controls. Payment data is handled entirely by Stripe/PayPal and never touches our servers.

4. Data Retention

We retain your data as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we permanently remove all personal data including: profile information, dog profiles, chat messages, photos, recordings, purchase history, reviews, and course progress.

5. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Rectify inaccurate data via your profile settings
  • Erase all your data by deleting your account (in-app or via our web page)
  • Data portability — contact us to receive your data in a portable format
  • Object to processing — contact us

6. Children's Privacy

Dogly Train is not intended for anyone under the age of 18. We verify age during registration and do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we discover that a user under 18 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it immediately.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Effective date" at the top of this page.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: doglytrain@gmail.com
